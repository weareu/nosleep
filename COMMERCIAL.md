# Commercial Licensing & Support

NoSleep is free and open source under **AGPL-3.0**. For personal use, or
inside a company on its own machines, you don't need anything else.

AGPL has one key condition. If you **modify NoSleep and let others use it
over a network** (a hosted service, a SaaS product, or an internal platform
for other teams), you must publish your modified source under AGPL too.

## Commercial license

If you can't meet AGPL's terms, for example you embed NoSleep in a
closed-source product or run a modified hosted service without publishing
changes, a **commercial license** is available. It also covers:

- warranty and indemnification your legal team may require,
- use of the NoSleep name for a hosted offering (see
  [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)).

## Support & services

- Priority support and setup help for teams running NoSleep
- Custom integrations (other agent CLIs, model providers, SSO)
- Sponsored features. The memory-at-scale work is the big one.

## Planned (not available yet)

- **NoSleep Relay:** a hosted relay so the official mobile app can reach your
  machine and get push notifications without Tailscale or your own Expo/EAS
  project.
- **Team edition:** multi-user auth, and shared strategy trees and Brain
  across a team.

If one of these would be useful to you, say so. Demand decides what gets
built first.
